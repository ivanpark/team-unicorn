# Engineering Thought — Team Unicorn Research Charter (v0.7.1)

**2026-10-13 · Conceptual/Metaphysical Concept Creation as an Engineering Thought Experiment**

## 0. Ultimate research purpose
Team Unicorn's primary object of inquiry is **the process through which abstract and metaphysical concepts are conceived, structured, made operational, tested and revised**—not the discovery of prime numbers, the creation of numbers, or a physical theory of the universe.

We choose the imagined engineering task of *implementing the already-defined abstract Number System Universe in a 3D/4D environment with explicit resource constraints* as a **testbed for studying conceptual construction**. A useful construction model does not prove that human thought literally works the same way.

## 1. Two levels of inquiry
- **Meta-level — thought engineering:** intuition → conceptual naming → constraints/invariants → generative rules → cost model → executable representation → observations and counterexamples → feedback → conceptual revision. Record failures as well as improvements.
- **Testbed — number-system implementation:** sequentially place integer stepping-stones through the successor +1; preserve inherited structural certificates; use prime-factor relations, 3D embeddings, gait/Fourier alternatives and intergenerational HQ learning as possible engineering methods.

**Non-negotiable:** unicorns are the *construction crew placing integer markers*. They are **not prime hunters**. Primes and Fourier tools are design hypotheses, not mission objectives.

## 2. Blueprint and conceptual-engineering correspondence
| Number-system blueprint layer | Construction task | Question about thought |
|---|---|---|
| Abstract Number System | Specify existing integers, rationals, reals and relations | What are the meanings and axiomatic commitments? |
| Phase I — Integer Foundation | Successor +1 markers, unbounded extension | What minimal manipulable representation establishes a concept? |
| Phase II — Structural Engineering | Inherited constructive checksum, reuse, relayout | How is prior knowledge certified, reused and reorganised? |
| Phase III — Numerical Continuum | Additional rules for representing between-marker quantities | How can finite symbols address open-ended possibilities? |
| Phase IV — Dynamic Mathematical Universe | Space, time, waves, feedback, propagation and morphogenesis | How do iterative revision and emergent organisation change a model? |

These are **interacting functional layers, not strictly successive time periods**. Phases I, II and IV operate concurrently.

## 3. Unicorn Relay
Unicorn U(t) knows its destination in advance and is designed at HQ. It reuses inherited information while travelling to the previous frontier, certifies the existing integer scaffold, places a final **+1** stone and reports construction and performance information back to HQ. HQ can improve the next unicorn's **number of legs (not necessarily prime)**, leg characteristics, frequency, phase, coordination and information costs.

The interval [n,n+1] becomes *addressable within the model* when the next marker is added; +1 does **not** create the real continuum or establish its completeness. Certification of a whole prefix and primality testing of its newest member are separate questions.

## 4. Operational measures for thought engineering
Compare rival representations on invariant preservation, testability, predictive utility, revision costs, provenance/information retention and sensitivity to counterexamples—not merely the number of ideas generated. Do not equate cognitive costs and physical resource units without a separate model.

1. Compare different representations that satisfy the same mathematical requirements.
2. Compare inherited proof/certificate reuse against full recomputation.
3. Compare fixed and adaptive feedback; centralised and local control.
4. Include shuffled/composite leg-label controls when testing prime significance.
5. Retain null and negative results without retroactively changing success criteria.

## 5. Entropy management
Information preservation, compression, erasure and updating may contribute engineering costs. Entropy is undefined for the integers *as such* without a probability ensemble. See [Entropy Management Protocol](Entropy_Management_v0.7.1_EN.md) for candidate quantities and constraints.

## 6. Evidence boundaries
- **Established mathematics:** successor arithmetic, unique factorisation, exact Fourier identities.
- **Current observations:** results from explicitly assumed toy thrust/cost models.
- **Speculative interpretation:** engineering optimality in physical 3D, physical meaning of prime-related waves, and an analogy to conceptual formation.

The toy universe is not evidence of a new law of mind, metaphysics or cosmology.
